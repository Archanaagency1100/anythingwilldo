At AWDORG Foundation,
we believe that true empowerment lies in education that uplifts both people and the planet. We provide free digital literacy programs to underprivileged children, helping them gain essential computer skills and confidence to thrive in a digital era. What sets us apart is our commitment to balancing ecology with empowerment — by encouraging individuals and organizations to donate their used (working) laptops and computers, we give technology a second life while reducing e-waste and environmental harm. You can also support our mission by donating under 80G, 12A, CSR-1, or FCRA, or by volunteering your time through our Time Share program to mentor and inspire young learners. Together, we’re shaping a future where education is sustainable, inclusive, and transformative.
